## Research Experience

### Undergraduate Research Intern
**Yonsei University College of Medicine** — Seoul, South Korea  
*Mar 2025 – Jun 2025*

- Investigated molecular mechanisms of SLC26A4-associated hearing loss using IVS7-2A>G and hH723R transgenic mouse models.
- Conducted Auditory Brainstem Response (ABR) testing and interpreted waveforms across 6–30 kHz to determine hearing thresholds and characterize auditory phenotypes in hH723R mutant mice.
- Troubleshot faint and non-specific PCR amplification by optimizing a touchdown PCR genotyping protocol.
- Performed cochlear microdissection and whole-mount immunofluorescence to evaluate hair-cell morphology and protein localization.
- Genotyped 150 transgenic mouse samples via DNA extraction and PCR for mutant identification.
