I am an M.S. student in Biomedical Engineering at Arizona State University, with a background in Biotechnology from Yonsei University.

My interests lie at the intersection of biomedical engineering, data analysis, and computational methods. I am particularly interested in applying quantitative approaches to biomedical and healthcare data, including biosignals, medical imaging, and other clinically relevant data.

I am currently developing my skills in Python-based data analysis and computational biomedical research through graduate coursework and projects, while exploring research opportunities in data-driven biomedical engineering.

## Research Interests

- Biomedical data analysis
- Biosignal and physiological data analysis
- Medical imaging and AI
- Computational methods for biomedical applications

## Current Focus

I am currently pursuing my M.S. in Biomedical Engineering at Arizona State University and building experience in computational and data-driven approaches to biomedical problems.
